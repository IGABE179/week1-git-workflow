Banana Pancakes

Soft pancakes made with ripe bananas, ready in 20 minutes.

Ingredients

  • 2 ripe bananas
  • 2 eggs
  • 1 cup of flour
  • 1/2 cup of milk
  • 1 teaspoon of baking powder
  • A pinch of salt

Steps

  1. Mash the bananas in a bowl.
  2. Add the eggs and milk and mix well.
  3. Stir in the flour, baking powder and salt.
  4. Heat a lightly oiled pan over medium heat.
  5. Pour small circles of batter and cook each side for 2 minutes.
  6. Serve warm.
